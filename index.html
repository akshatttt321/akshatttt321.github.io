Trading Agent

An autonomous agent that trades Hyperliquid perps and spot plus Polymarket prediction markets. Two language models argue over every trade, and a deterministic risk layer has the final say.

Gemini / OpenAI / AnthropicHyperliquid SDKpy-clob-clientSQLiteDockerCaddy
Source ↗
  • Two-model loop. A proposer emits a structured decision; an independent verifier with a skeptic prompt must approve anything that adds risk. Vetoes are journaled.
  • Hard risk gate. Leverage and exposure caps, mandatory stop-loss, order cooldown, a daily-loss halt and a drawdown kill that flattens everything and refuses to restart.
  • Online learner. Each closed trade becomes an R-multiple reward for its setup context, which scales future position size between 0.25× and 1×.
  • Safe to deploy. One-command VPS deploy runs preflight checks inside the container first. A static dashboard shows equity, the decision feed and a kill switch.

Manhunt

Real-world hide and seek for phones. Runners hide inside a zone that shrinks over the game, hunters see everyone on a timed GPS ping, and tags are confirmed by proximity. One codebase for Android and iOS.

FlutterSupabasePostgreSQLOpenStreetMapGeolocator
Source available on request
  • Host-tuned rules. Duration, ping interval, head start, zone radius, shrink stages, grace period and tag distance are all set per game.
  • Shrinking zone. Runners caught outside get a warning and a countdown, then disqualification when the grace period ends.
  • Fair tagging. A hunter in range gets a Tag button. The runner confirms or disputes within 15 seconds, and the host rules on disputes.
  • Offline practice. Bots that move, ping and tag, plus per-game results and monthly and lifetime leaderboards.
